🕉️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Subah Ki Aarti ("the App") is a devotional application designed to help users with their daily spiritual practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects minimal information necessary for its functionality:

  • Alarm Settings: Your alarm times and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • App Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience
  • Device Information: Basic device information for compatibility purposes

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Financial information

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Provide alarm and notification functionality
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: For analytics and app performance monitoring.

4. Data Storage

All your personal preferences (alarms, settings) are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store any personal data on external servers.

5. Advertisements

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the application
  • Opt out of personalized advertisements
  • Request information about data we have collected

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
